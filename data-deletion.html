User Data Deletion

How to request deletion of personal information processed by UlvornApps.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

If you believe UlvornApps has processed your personal information and you would like to request deletion, email us at [REPLACE WITH OFFICIAL PRIVACY EMAIL] with the subject line “UlvornApps Data Deletion Request.”

What to include

To help us locate the relevant information and protect against unauthorized requests, please include:

  • Your name and the email address or phone number connected to the request;
  • The name of the business that used UlvornApps to communicate with you, if known;
  • A description of the data or interaction you want deleted; and
  • Any relevant WhatsApp phone number or message date.

What happens next

We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and authority. After verification, we will review the request and delete eligible information from our active systems, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention needs (for example, legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution obligations). We will respond using the contact information in your request.

WhatsApp and other parties

Some information may be controlled by the business that communicated with you or by Meta/WhatsApp. Where a request relates to information outside our control, we may direct you to the appropriate party.
