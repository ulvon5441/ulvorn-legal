Last updated: 6 October 2026

Caretutors Technologies Ltd (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates UlvornApps. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with UlvornApps, including its integration with the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Depending on how the service is configured and used, we may process business account and integration information, such as WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers, message-template metadata, recipient contact information supplied by our customers, message content or delivery status, and technical records needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

We use this information to provide and support the service; send, receive, or manage WhatsApp business communications at a customer’s direction; maintain service security and reliability; investigate faults or misuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including to provide the service, resolve issues, and satisfy legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.
